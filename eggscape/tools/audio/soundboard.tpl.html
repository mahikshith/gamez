Eggscape Sound Board

Every sound in the game, played by the game's own sound engine. Turn your phone's volume up and tap. Mark anything you dislike with the "No" button and the list at the bottom fills in.

Main theme

The home screen and the level map play it, every world quotes its first two bars once a loop, and the level-win fanfare is built on it. On the farm it changes costume from level to level.

World music

Every world now has its own written tune that starts with a fanfare at full volume. "New" plays it; "Level 2" is how the next level in that world starts (another lead or key); "Boss" is the boss-level version; "Old" is the music you heard before. Each tune is about 30 seconds and changes its arrangement the second time round.
