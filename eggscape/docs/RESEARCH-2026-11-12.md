# Eggscape: quality-bar research (12 Nov 2026)

## 0. Method and honesty note
- **REA ran here** (`npx rea-agents@latest`, v6.9.0). Used: `list-browser-targets`, `inspect-web-page`, `analyze-web-bundle` (headless Chrome over CDP) and `analyze-javascript-application` (Stickman Hook bundle: 492 modules, 216,453 AST nodes, evidence `ev_a28097…`). REA's native/APK engines (Ghidra, JADX, apktool) are not installed and no leader APK could be lawfully fetched (APKPure 403, github.com/morluto/rea 403 through the proxy), so **Android leaders were studied from public listings and articles only, not binaries**.
- REA's web capture only saw inline scripts on cross-origin game frames, so transfer sizes and asset lists were measured with a small CDP network recorder of my own plus `curl`. Technique only; no code or assets were kept for reuse.
- Labels below: **[M]** measured by me, **[S]** stated by a source, **[J]** my judgement/estimate.

Targets: Stickman Hook, Drive Mad, Monkey Mart (Poki); Cut the Rope licensed web build (CrazyGames); Play listings of Cut the Rope, Angry Birds 2, Candy Crush Saga, Where's My Water? 2, Royal Match.

## 1. Asset and animation bar

**What the screenshots show [J].** The Godot app (0.4) already reads cleaner than the web build: big character, bright flat scenes, few words. The web build is where "boxy and kiddish" comes from: near-black rectangular modals with thin outlines over sunny scenes, a grey HUD band with small low-contrast text ("need 2"), every button the same flat pill in primary blue/green/yellow, text-box tutorials, and a hen about 6% of screen height. Rings use one stroke weight, so 6-ring levels turn into visual noise.

**What comparables do [M].**
| Game | Tech | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Stickman Hook | PIXI + GSAP, 1.6 MB JS | Character is a 1024x1024 sheet of ~19 hand-drawn poses, thick outline plus specular dot; FX and props on 512/1024 power-of-two sheets; two display fonts (Odin Rounded Bold, Junegull), no system font; soft drop shadow sprite under the character, motion trail, confetti, camera zoom and x-offset with smooth damping |
| Cut the Rope (web) | Custom Haxe WebGL engine, 459 kB JS | Shaders with colour multiplier/offset uniforms, DXT texture support, intro video scene, 425 levels in boxes of 25, seasons |
| Monkey Mart | Defold wasm, 6.5 MB | Title as a 3D-ish wooden sign logo, characters with 2-tone shading and ground shadows |
| Drive Mad | Fancade wasm, ~5.4 MB decoded | Minimal flat style, quality carried by physics feel |

None of these four ships Spine; they use pose sheets plus tweens. Spine/mesh rigs in Royal Match, Candy Crush and Angry Birds 2 are widely reported but **I could not verify them from binaries**.

**15 checkable techniques for Eggscape (Canvas 2D and Godot)**
1. Replace near-black modals with light, theme-tinted cards: 28-32 px corner radius, 2-tone body, soft 12-16 px drop shadow, no 1 px outlines.
2. One primary button style only (large, glossy top highlight, darker bottom lip, 6 px press-down travel); all secondary actions become round icon buttons. Max one primary per screen.
3. Display font with outline plus drop shadow for titles and numbers; a second rounded font for body. Never below 14 px at 412 px width. Logo drawn as artwork, not typed text.
4. Fixed palette per world: 1 hue family, 1 accent, and UI colours shared across all worlds. Remove pure primary blue.
5. Two ring stroke weights and a faint inner highlight plus contact shadow, so rings read as physical tubes; fade non-interactive rings to 60%.
6. Hen at 18-25% of screen height on menu/result screens, with idle blink (every 3-5 s), breathing scale (1.00-1.03, 2 s loop), anticipation and reaction poses (6-10 poses is enough; Stickman Hook ships ~19).
7. Squash and stretch on eggs: scale along velocity up to 1.15, squash to 0.8 for 80 ms on landing.
8. Ground shadow ellipse under every egg and character.
9. Easing everywhere: UI enters with back-out 250-350 ms, exits 150 ms; nothing appears on a single frame.
10. Hit-stop 60-90 ms and 4-6 px screen shake decaying over 200 ms on egg break and boss hit; respect the existing reduced-motion toggle.
11. Particles from 3-4 small pre-rendered soft textures (dot, star, shell shard, feather) drawn additively; 12-20 per event, cap ~150 live.
12. Camera: 1.0-1.5 s intro pan from nest up to the eggs, slight zoom-in (1.05) on the last egg, slow-motion 0.4x for 300 ms on the winning drop.
13. Result screen choreography: stars land one by one (120 ms apart, scale 1.6 to 1.0, sound pitch rising), score counts up, coins fly to the counter.
14. Parallax background in 3 layers with a subtle vignette; drop the brick/dirt slab under the playfield.
15. Pre-render code-drawn art once to offscreen canvases at device pixel ratio (cap 2x) so outlines stay crisp and frame time stays flat.

## 2. Download size: is 27 MB "too low"?
No. It is an advantage.
- Google's own study: **every +6 MB of APK costs about 1% install conversion** (under 100 MB); removing 10 MB lifts conversion ~2.5% in India/Brazil [S: medium.com/googleplaydev/shrinking-apks-growing-installs-5d3fcba23ce2, repeated at developer.android.com/games/optimize/game-size].
- Royal Match APK variants are 186-208 MB (APKMirror, May 2024) [S]. Big size comes from thousands of levels, events and Spine art, not from quality per se. Players never see the number before judging the icon and screenshots [J].
- Web: CrazyGames allows 50 MB initial download but **only 20 MB to be eligible for the mobile homepage**, 250 MB total, 1,500 files, gameplay within 20 s [S: docs.crazygames.com/requirements/technical]. Poki says target **under 8 MB initial**, and that each extra MB costs "a couple percent" of players [S: sdk.poki.com/new-requirements, poki.com/blog/building-web-browser-games-2026].
- Measured transfers: Stickman Hook 1.0 MB, Monkey Mart 6.5 MB, Drive Mad ~5.4 MB decoded [M].
Target [J]: web under 5 MB initial; Android 25-60 MB. Spend added megabytes on audio and character art, never on padding.

## 3. Android monetization, without hype
**Hard facts**
- All five leaders are free. Four show ads plus IAP ($0.49-$249.99 per item); Royal Match is IAP only. "Cut the Rope GOLD" at $0.99 has 1M+ installs versus 100M+ for the free one, and still carries ads and IAP [M: Play listings].
- Paid titles are 3.6% of all store titles; Appfigures' answer to "ship a paid app in 2026?" is no [S: appfigures.com/resources/insights/20251121].
- Android eCPM, games, Q2 2024 (Tenjin x CAS): US rewarded $30, interstitial $16; Canada $23/$13. Appodeal Q4 2024 regional: Europe ~$5/$3.6, Latin America ~$1.8/$1.3 [S, via blog.playio.co/mobile-game-ecpm-benchmarks-2026]. Sources differ up to 4x; small unmediated apps sit at the low end [J].
- Retention across mobile games (GameAnalytics 2026): top quartile D1 ~30%, D7 6-7%, D30 1.6-1.8%; median D7 under 4% [S: gamedevreports.substack.com].
- Remove-ads IAP: Breakout Ninja converted 0.08% ($1.1k IAP vs $23k ads on 650k downloads, 2017) [S: gamedeveloper.com]. Commonly quoted 0.5-2% is unverified.
- Play Pass: invitation/nomination, paid by subscriber time, rates undisclosed [S]. Do not plan on it.
- Licensed Cut the Rope web config: interstitial cooldown 60 s at level-end/menu events, rewarded only on two power-ups, no banner [M: famobi.json].

**Realistic arithmetic for an unknown solo dev [J, estimates]**
- Organic installs with no marketing: tens to low hundreds per month; a good listing plus web-portal cross-promotion may reach 1-5k/month. Most unknown games never pass 10k total.
- Ad revenue per install, rewarded plus light interstitials, global mix: $0.02-$0.10; US-heavy: $0.20-$0.50.
- So 10,000 installs is roughly $200-$1,000 in ads plus $50-$300 from a $2.99 remove-ads at 0.3-1%. A $1.99 paid app would get perhaps 1-3% of the installs a free one gets: 100-300 sales, about $170-$500 after the 15% fee, and zero cross-promotion value.

| Model | Risk |
|---|---|
| Paid upfront | Almost no discovery, refunds, piracy; kills the web-to-app funnel |
| Ads only | Income scales only with volume; tier-3 traffic pays cents |
| Free + remove-ads/premium | Low conversion, but costs nothing to add |
| Hybrid (recommended) | More SDK and consent (UMP/GDPR) work |

**Recommendation.** Free app; rewarded ads on the hooks the web build already has (hint, double coins, extra egg, rewind); interstitials only between levels, 60-90 s cooldown, none before level 6; one **$2.99-$3.99 "Premium"** (no interstitials, a daily free hint, exclusive skin); 2-3 coin packs. Treat Android as an add-on to web-portal income for the first year, not as the income. The lever that changes the numbers is installs and D1 retention, not the pricing model.

## 4. Store listing bar and asset checklist
Google Play rules [S: support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151]: icon 512x512 32-bit PNG, max 1024 KB; feature graphic 1024x500 JPEG/24-bit PNG no alpha; 2-8 screenshots per device type, 320-3840 px, long side at most 2x short side; **games need at least three 9:16 (min 1080x1920) or 16:9 (min 1920x1080) shots to be eligible for featured formats**; video is a YouTube URL, not monetized, first 30 s autoplays, gameplay within 10 s; no "Best/#1/Free/Play now" text.
Leaders [M]: all five have a video; Royal Match ships 15 portrait shots at 1729x3074; Candy Crush portrait 1200x1920; Cut the Rope 2048x2732 and 1290x2796; Angry Birds 2 landscape 2208x1242.
Portals [S]: CrazyGames covers 1920x1080, 800x1200, 800x800, title on cover in a stylised font, no borders, no extra words; silent 15-20 s preview in 1080p 16:9 and 2:3, under 50 MB. Poki: full-bleed square at least 628x628, **no text**, one character in a dynamic pose, avoid colours near #83FFE7, plus a short hover video. Poki serves tiles at 188x188, so test at that size [M].

**Produce**
- [ ] Icon 512x512 (master 1024x1024): hen face plus egg, no text, readable at 48 px
- [ ] Feature graphic 1024x500 (logo left, hen and ring right, nothing vital in outer 15%)
- [ ] 8 phone screenshots 1080x1920 portrait: 5-word caption band on top, one idea each (core mechanic, boss, worlds, power-ups, skins, daily, 3-star result, world map)
- [ ] 4 tablet screenshots 1600x2560 (7-inch and 10-inch slots)
- [ ] Trailer 20-30 s, 1080x1920, gameplay from second 1, YouTube unlisted, monetization off
- [ ] CrazyGames covers 1920x1080, 800x1200, 800x800 plus videos 1920x1080 and 1080x1620, 15-20 s, silent
- [ ] Poki thumbnail 628x628 (master 1024x1024), text-free, plus 3-5 s hover clip
- [ ] Short description (80 chars max), logo as transparent PNG 2000 px wide

## 5. First-time experience
Patterns: play within seconds, teach by doing, guaranteed early wins, fast retries, a visible next goal. Poki: "the first couple of seconds decide whether players stay"; text tutorials make players leave; build portrait first [S]. Royal Match skips the objective screen on retry and puts a breather level every 10 [S: medium.com/ironsource-levelup].

**Web (portal) [J]**
1. Tap-to-play straight into level 1; no menu, difficulty picker or shop on first visit.
2. Replace the "The Farm" text card with a ghost hand dragging the ring; dismiss on first successful drag.
3. Level 1 unlosable, won in under 20 s; levels 1-3 always 3 stars.
4. Delete Easy/Medium/Hard from the first screen; adapt silently after two fails.
5. Retry in under 1 s, no modal; failure screen offers one rewarded "save the egg".
6. No rewarded prompts before level 3; first boss by minute 4-5 as the hook.
7. Initial download under 5 MB; lazy-load worlds 2-10.
8. Fire gameplayStart/Stop and happy-moment SDK events correctly; portals rank on playtime and return rate.
9. HUD shows only eggs needed and timer; move score to the result screen.

**App [J]**
1. Cold start to playable under 5 s; no login, no consent wall before level 1 (show UMP after level 2).
2. World map as a path with the hen walking node to node, not a grid of numbered tiles.
3. Daily egg with a 7-day streak calendar; notification permission asked only after the first boss win.
4. Star chests every 15 stars; a free skin at level 5 so the shop opens with something owned.
5. Fill the empty white result screen: animated hen, star choreography, "next" preview.
6. Haptics on ring detents, egg landing and star hits.
7. Breather/bonus level every 10; boss intro as a 3 s cinematic, not a paragraph.
8. Offline play with local save plus Play Games cloud save.
9. Ask for a rating after a 3-star boss win, never after a fail.
10. Ship "coming soon" worlds only when real; locked content on the second screen reads as unfinished.
